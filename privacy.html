Privacy Policy

Last updated November 1, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Morgan USA LLC, doing business as Tactical Si ("Tactical Si"), collects, uses and protects information when you visit tacticalsi.us (the "Site"), sign up for the Dominate package or contact Tactical Si.

Information Tactical Si collects

  • Sign-up and business details: your name, business name, email, Google listing link or business address, services, hours, photos and logo, domain and registrar details, and, if you add them, your website and any notes.
  • Payment information: payments are handled by Stripe. Tactical Si receives confirmation of payment, your name, email and billing details, but not your full card number.
  • Messages: what you send Tactical Si by email or through the chat on the Site, and Tactical Si's replies.
  • Site usage: basic technical information that web servers and browsers record, such as IP address, browser type, pages visited and the time of your visit.
  • Public business information: to deliver the services, Tactical Si looks at public information about your business and nearby competitors, such as Google listings, reviews and AI search answers.

How Tactical Si uses it

  • To deliver the services and to answer your questions.
  • To process payments and refunds and keep business and tax records.
  • To send occasional emails about Tactical Si's services. Every marketing email has an unsubscribe link, and you can also email Tactical Si to stop them.
  • To run, secure and improve the Site, and to meet legal obligations.

How Tactical Si shares it

Tactical Si does not sell your personal information. Tactical Si shares it only with service providers that help run the business, such as its payment processor (Stripe), website hosting (GitHub Pages), its customer relationship management, chat and email tools, domain registrar, and the software partners used to deliver the services. They may use it only to provide services to Tactical Si. Tactical Si may also disclose information if the law requires it or to protect its rights or someone's safety, or as part of a sale or reorganization of the business, subject to this policy.

Text messages

Tactical Si doesn't send text messages for the Dominate package on tacticalsi.us. No mobile information will be shared with third parties for marketing or promotional purposes.

Cookies

The Site does not use advertising cookies. Tactical Si's payment processor may use cookies on its checkout page to process payments and prevent fraud. You can control cookies in your browser settings.

How long Tactical Si keeps it

Tactical Si keeps information only as long as it reasonably needs it to deliver the services, keep business and financial records, resolve disputes and meet legal requirements. Then Tactical Si deletes it or makes it anonymous.

Security

Tactical Si uses reasonable safeguards to protect your information. No method of sending or storing data online is completely secure, so Tactical Si can't guarantee absolute security.

Your choices

You can ask Tactical Si what personal information it has about you, or ask it to correct or delete it, subject to records Tactical Si must keep by law. Email hello@tacticalsi.us. Tactical Si may need to confirm your identity first.

Children

The Site and Tactical Si's services are for businesses and adults. Tactical Si doesn't knowingly collect information from children under 13.

Changes to this policy

Tactical Si may update this policy from time to time. Tactical Si will post the new version on this page and change the date above.
