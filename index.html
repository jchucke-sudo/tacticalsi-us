Plain-English answers

Questions people ask

I'm not a tech person. Is that a problem?

No. Your steps are short and you're walked through each one, on a screen share if you want. Tactical Si does the technical work.

Does Tactical Si need my passwords?

No. Tactical Si never asks for, stores or shares your passwords. You add Tactical Si as a helper on your registrar account and as Manager on your Google profile.

Who owns my domain?

You do, from day one. It's registered in your name in your own account. You can get your login and transfer code the same business day you ask, at any time.

I already have a domain or a website. Can I still do this?

Yes. If you already own a domain, you keep it and just add Tactical Si as a helper. Nothing is transferred to Tactical Si.

Can Tactical Si guarantee I'll rank or get calls?

No, and nobody honestly can. Google decides rankings. What Tactical Si commits to is the work in this package, done every month, and a monthly report so you can see what's happening.

What if I want to leave before month 12?

You can. Leaving early means a buyout or the remaining months, as set out in the agreement, which you see before you sign. Your domain and Google profile stay yours either way. What happens to the website if you leave early is covered on the kickoff call and set out in the agreement.

What happens at month 12?

The website becomes yours. You'll get a reminder about 45 days before. What happens after month 12 is covered on the kickoff call.

Is there a setup fee?

Covered on the kickoff call.

Will Tactical Si text my customers?

No. Texting isn't part of this package. Review requests and customer emails go by email.

When does work start?

After the agreement is signed and the first $799 payment clears. Nothing is built or bought before that.
