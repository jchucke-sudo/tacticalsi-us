Refund & Cancellation Policy

Last updated November 1, 2026

This policy is offered by Morgan USA LLC, doing business as Tactical Si ("Tactical Si"), and covers the Dominate 12-month package sold on tacticalsi.us: $799 per month for 12 consecutive months, $9,588 in total.

A 12-month commitment, not month to month

The tacticalsi.us package is a 12-month commitment. The website build and the domain registration fee for the 12 months carry no separate charge only because of that commitment. Nothing is built, bought or switched on until the agreement is signed and the first $799 payment clears.

Monthly payments

Monthly payments aren't refunded for partial months.

If something is wrong with the work

If the work is for the wrong business or is missing a part described in the Terms of Service, tell Tactical Si within 14 days and Tactical Si will fix it. Tactical Si doesn't give refunds because a ranking, a number of calls or an AI tool's answer didn't change.

How to ask for a refund

Email hello@tacticalsi.us from the email you signed up with, or include it in your message, along with your business name. Tactical Si will confirm by email. Approved refunds go back to the card you paid with through Stripe. Your bank decides how long it takes to show up, usually a few business days.

Charged by mistake?

If you were charged twice or by mistake, email Tactical Si and it will be fixed.

Leaving before month 12

To ask to leave early, email hello@tacticalsi.us with your business name. Tactical Si will confirm by email. Leaving before month 12 means a buyout or the remaining months, as set out in the agreement, which you see before you sign. What happens to the website if you leave early is covered on the kickoff call and set out in the agreement.

What you keep, whatever happens

  • Your domain. It's registered in your name from day one. On request, Tactical Si hands over your login details and transfer code the same business day, even if you leave early and even if a balance is due.
  • Your Google Business Profile, reviews, photos and business content. They stay yours. Tactical Si's Manager access is removed when services end.
  • Your website becomes yours when the 12 months are complete.

At month 12

About 45 days before your 12 months end, Tactical Si reminds you, so nothing renews or ends by surprise. What happens after month 12 is covered on the kickoff call.
